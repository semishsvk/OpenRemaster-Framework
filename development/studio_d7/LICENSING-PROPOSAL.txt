Proposed Studio license

MIT is proposed for the original OpenRemaster Studio source and build scripts.
The OSI-approved text is available at https://opensource.org/license/mit.
It permits use, modification and redistribution, including commercial use,
subject to preserving the copyright and permission notice.

Approval is pending from the copyright holders. The copyright attribution,
the covered source paths and the rights in contributed files need confirmation.
After approval, publish the agreed LICENSE text and update package metadata.

Third-party components retain their respective licenses. Game screenshots,
game data, logos and other media require their own distribution permissions.
The dependency inventory and copied notices in a DEV archive support review.
The recorded gaps in that inventory require resolution before SignPath intake.

This document records a proposal. The repository's existing rights and any
permissions already granted by the copyright holders govern this DEV preview.
